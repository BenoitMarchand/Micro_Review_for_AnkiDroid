## Full description (4,000 characters max)

```
Learn a little every time you pick up your phone. Micro Review for AnkiDroid shows the next card of your AnkiDroid decks where you already look: on your lock screen, in a notification and in a home screen widget. Answer it in a few seconds, without opening AnkiDroid.

REVIEW FROM THE LOCK SCREEN
• Set the Micro Review wallpaper on your lock screen: the question of the next card is shown there, in big letters. Your home screen wallpaper doesn't change. Where a live wallpaper can't be set on the lock screen alone (before Android 14, some devices), a picture of the card is set there instead, changed with each card.
• Choose its look: background color or your own picture (darkened, blurred, with a gradient or a panel behind the text so it stays readable), text color and size, question only or question and answer.

ANSWER FROM A NOTIFICATION
• The card's notification is visible on the lock screen: expand it, tap Show answer, then Again, Hard, Good or Easy. Only the buttons the card actually has are shown.
• One card per unlock: after your answer, the next card waits for your next unlock. A small habit, never a chore. Prefer longer sessions? Turn on "Review several cards in a row".

HOME SCREEN WIDGET
• Add the Card widget and tap Start review: question, Show answer, answer buttons, then the next card right away.

YOUR DECKS, YOUR ORDER
• Pick the decks to review and put them in order: cards come from the first deck that has one to review, then the next one. A deck includes its subdecks.
• Optional reminders at the times you choose.

YOUR DATA STAYS IN ANKIDROID
• Micro Review stores no cards and no reviews of its own: everything is read from and written to AnkiDroid through its official API, so your reviews count in AnkiDroid, its statistics and its sync.
• No account, no ads, no tracking, no internet access.

REQUIREMENTS
• AnkiDroid installed, with its API enabled (AnkiDroid: Settings › Advanced › Enable AnkiDroid API).
• Live lock screen wallpaper: Android 14 or later with a lock screen wallpaper picker; elsewhere, a picture of the card (it replaces your lock screen picture). Lock screen features that change the picture by themselves (Magazine unlock, Glance) must be off.
• Cards with sound or images are shown as text; cards with sound in the question are skipped (they stay due in AnkiDroid).

Micro Review for AnkiDroid is an independent open source app. It is not made or endorsed by the AnkiDroid team.
```
