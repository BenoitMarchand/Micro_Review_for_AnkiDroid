# Privacy policy: Micro Review for AnkiDroid

Last updated: see this file's history.

Micro Review for AnkiDroid ("the app") lets you review the flashcards of the AnkiDroid app from your lock screen, a notification and a home screen widget.

## Data the app uses

- **Your flashcards and reviews**: the app reads the cards of the decks you select, and records your answers, through the official API of AnkiDroid installed on your phone. This data stays in AnkiDroid, on your phone. The app keeps no copy, except the card currently shown (its question and answer), so that it can be displayed after a restart.
- **Your settings**: selected decks and their order, review settings, lock screen style and the background picture you choose. They are stored in the app's private storage on your phone, and included in Android's backup of the app if you have backups turned on.

## Data the app does not collect

The app has no internet access (it doesn't ask for the permission). It collects no personal data, sends nothing to its developers or to anyone else, contains no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## Reporting an issue

Settings › Help › "Report an issue" opens a GitHub issue form in your browser, pre-filled with your phone model, Android version and the versions of the app and of AnkiDroid. Nothing is sent unless you submit the form yourself on GitHub, under GitHub's own privacy policy.

## Permissions

- **AnkiDroid database access**: read your decks and cards, and record your answers.
- **Notifications**: show the card to review.
- **Run at startup**: show the card again after a restart, and keep the review reminders.
- **Set wallpaper** (optional, only when you choose the card picture): put a picture of the current card on your lock screen. Your previous lock screen picture is not read or kept.
- **Display over other apps** (optional, only for "Open after unlock"): open the review screen when you unlock your phone.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes and contact

This policy may change with new versions of the app; changes are published in this file. Questions: open an issue on the app's GitHub repository.
